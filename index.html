How to Play

🚶
Move
Tap/swipe on the map or use the D-pad to move. Enemies move when you move.
⚔
Fight
Walk into an enemy to attack it. Each enemy has HP shown above it.
🍴
Items
Walk over potions to heal and gold to increase your score.
🚪
Descend
Find the stairs on each floor to go deeper. Enemies get tougher.
💾
Save
Your game is saved every move. Close and come back anytime.
